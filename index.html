Method. Posts mentioning Mike Alfred on X are classified daily as negative, neutral or positive by an automated reader (the Grok bot), judging sentiment toward him personally. The negative score (0–100) is the share of classified posts that are negative. The engagement-weighted version gives more weight to posts with more likes, reposts and replies.

Coverage. “Full capture” means every post X search returns for the day was classified, after removing spam and duplicates. X's own daily count, shown where available, runs higher: on the days measured so far, search returned 62–91% of it (the day notes give the shortfall as a share of X's count). The likely reason is posts search no longer returns, such as deleted posts or protected accounts, but that has not been verified.

Sampled days. Days drawn dashed on a shaded background classified only a sample of 20–50 posts, mostly top-ranked, and were labelled by an earlier keyword method that reads lower than reading each post, so they are not comparable with full-capture days. They are also much noisier: one post moves that day's score by 2–5 points. They get no band label and do not set the chart's scale; a ▲ marks any value above the top of the chart.

7-day average. Uses only days captured the same way and skips unfinished days and days with no posts, so it restarts where coverage changes. At the start of a run it averages fewer than seven days; the tooltip shows how many.

Days run midnight to midnight US Eastern. Data refreshes once a day. Not investment advice.
